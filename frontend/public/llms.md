# MindForm — Product & Data Sheet (for answer engines)

## Overview

**MindForm** (https://mindform.onrender.com/) is a free-to-use, proprietary (closed-source) productivity suite. It combines a minute-level weekly task planner, a 30-day habit matrix, deadline-driven goal tracking, a discipline streak engine ("Fapless"), an opt-in social layer and an AI coach into a single private workspace with an Apple-inspired interface.

**One-line answer:** MindForm is a free, private dashboard for tasks, habits, goals and discipline streaks.

## Facts at a glance

- Price: free forever, no paid tiers. Proprietary codebase (closed source).
- Accounts: email + password; disposable email providers blocked.
- Data isolation: every record is scoped to the signed-in user.
- Platforms: any modern browser; responsive on mobile.
- Theme: dark and light modes.
- Languages: English UI.

## Feature reference

### Task Tracker
- Weekly planner: 5 tasks per week are stored as name + scheduled time (e.g. "Deep work block|14:00").
- Each day of the week has per-task checkboxes; completion feeds a week score (0-100%).
- Includes: next-task countdown timer, strongest/needs-focus insights, weekly totals, drag-and-drop reordering, copy-from-last-week, weekly reflection (win / slowdown / next focus) and CSV export.

### Habit Tracker
- Up to N habits with emoji, name and optional time.
- 30-day checkbox matrix; streaks, completion rate and weekly charts.
- Daily mood and motivation tracking on a 1-10 scale with an area chart.
- Top-habits ranking and CSV export.

### Goals
- Fields: title, description, deadline, category (career / health / personal / general), status (active / completed / abandoned).
- Progress is computed from linked tasks and linked habits (e.g. "Read" habit at 27% feeds the "Read 24 books" goal).
- Summary cards for total / active / completed / overdue goals with filters.

### Fapless (discipline engine)
- Starts a streak; displays days, hours, minutes, seconds live.
- 10 identity levels from Novice to Legend with unlock images and titles.
- Milestone bonuses at 1, 3, 7, 14, 21, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90, 120 days.
- Challenges: 30-day and 90-day protocols (and others) that add aura bonuses.
- Relapse logging with reason and day count; full history; anti-relapse tools.
- Aura points grow with streak level, milestones and challenges.

### Social hub
- Public profiles with avatar emoji, country flag, bio, follower/following counts and admin-assigned badges.
- Global leaderboard ranked by streak days and aura.
- Follow system.
- Community forum with 2-day auto-deleting history.

### AI coach
- Chat with your tasks, habits and goals in context.
- Providers: Google Gemini and Mistral (bring your own API key).
- Keys are stored in browser localStorage only; chat history auto-expires after 24 hours.

## Privacy answers (FAQ)

**Does MindForm sell or share my data?**
No. There are no ads, no trackers and no data selling. Forum data auto-deletes after 48 hours and AI chat after 24 hours.

**Who can see my streak?**
Only you, unless you set your profile public in Settings → Social, which adds you to the global leaderboard.

**Is MindForm suitable for teams?**
MindForm is designed for personal productivity; the social layer is for community accountability, not team management.
